# Phase 3: Google Ads

**Weeks 5–6** · 10 lessons, 1 weekly review, 1 checkpoint

## What you'll be able to do

- Explain how the Google Ads auction ranks ads and sets prices.
- Research keywords, choose match types, and structure an account.
- Write responsive search ads and assets within platform limits.
- Choose a bid strategy and calculate budgets for a goal.
- Build a launch-ready campaign offline and check it against a pre-launch list.
- Find wasted spend, diagnose performance, and write a prioritized optimization plan.
- Explain when to use Performance Max, Shopping, Display, YouTube, and remarketing.

## Before you start

Lesson 21 walks you through creating a Google Ads account without a campaign. You don't need to
enter card details. Every practice has a fallback if you can't create an account.

## Week 5 review

**Saturday** · 30–45 minutes · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/week-05-review.md`, using
[`templates/weekly-review.md`](../../templates/weekly-review.md)

Answer from memory first, then check against the lessons.

1. Explain why a lower bid can win a higher position than a bigger bid.
2. What does a "Below average" landing page experience tell you, and what would you fix?
3. Give one keyword for each search intent for your practice business.
4. Why might "best" be a dangerous negative keyword?
5. Read your RSA from practice 24 aloud in three random combinations. Does each make sense?
6. Your campaign has 12 conversions a month. Is a target CPA strategy a good idea yet? Why or
   why not?
7. Calculate the daily budget for a $3,000 monthly budget.

Then catch up on any unfinished practice from the week.
