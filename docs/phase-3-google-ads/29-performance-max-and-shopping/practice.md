# Practice 29: Plan a product feed and a Performance Max campaign

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Google Sheets · **Save to:** A sheet named `Lesson 29 - PMax` in your Drive course folder. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-29.md`.

## Goal

Write product feed rows that match searches well, and plan a Performance Max campaign.

If your practice business sells a service, not products, do Part 1 for Pebble Coffee Co. and
Part 2 for your own business.

## Steps

### Part 1: Product feed (12 minutes)

1. Create a tab named `feed` with columns: `id`, `title`, `description`, `link`, `image_link`,
   `price`, `availability`, `brand`.
2. Write 5 product rows. Make up sensible values where needed.
3. Write each `title` in this pattern: **brand + product type + key attributes + size**. For
   example: `Pebble Ethiopia Coffee Beans, Light Roast, Whole Bean, 250 g`.
4. Check each title against your keyword list from practice 22. Does it contain the words
   people search?
5. Rewrite this weak title into a strong one: `Yirgacheffe 250`.

### Part 2: Performance Max plan (18 minutes)

1. Create a tab named `pmax`. Plan **2 asset groups**, each for one theme, such as "Gifts" and
   "Subscription".
2. For each asset group, write:
   - 5 headlines, up to 30 characters
   - 2 long headlines, up to 90 characters
   - 2 descriptions, up to 90 characters, with at least one up to 60 characters
   - A list of 3 image ideas: landscape, square, and portrait
   - 1 video idea, in one sentence
   - Audience signals: your own data, such as site visitors or a customer list, plus 5 search
     terms for a custom segment
3. Write the goal, the target, and two exclusions you'd add, such as your brand name.
4. Write 2–3 sentences: How would you check whether PMax brings new customers, not only people
   who'd have bought anyway?

## Done when

- [ ] 5 feed rows have descriptive titles in the recommended pattern.
- [ ] The weak title is rewritten.
- [ ] 2 asset groups are planned with every part filled in.
- [ ] The goal, target, exclusions, and new-customer check are written.

## Check yourself

1. Why is the product title the most important feed field?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Shopping has no keywords. Google matches searches mainly using the title, so the words in it
   decide which searches the product can appear for.
   </details>

2. What does an audience signal do in PMax?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   It gives Google a starting hint about likely customers. It isn't strict targeting: Google can
   show ads to people outside it.
   </details>

3. PMax reports a ROAS of 8, but total store revenue didn't change after launch. What might be
   happening?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   PMax may be claiming credit for sales that would have happened anyway, such as brand searches
   or returning customers. It looks efficient without adding new sales.
   </details>
