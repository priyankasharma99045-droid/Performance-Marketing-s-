## Part 2: Phase quiz (15–20 minutes)

Answer from memory in `my-work/notes/week-06-review.md`, then check.

1. Name four factors that decide Ad Rank.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Any four of: bid, ad and landing page quality, Ad Rank thresholds, auction competitiveness,
   search context, and expected impact of assets.
   </details>

2. What are the three components of Quality Score?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience.
   </details>

3. Explain broad, phrase, and exact match in one sentence each.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Broad: searches related to the keyword's meaning. Phrase: searches that include the
   keyword's meaning. Exact: searches with the same meaning as the keyword.
   </details>

4. When do you split into a new campaign, and when into a new ad group?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   A new campaign for different budgets or settings. A new ad group for different ads.
   </details>

5. How many headlines and descriptions can an RSA have, and what are their character limits?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Up to 15 headlines of 30 characters, and up to 4 descriptions of 90 characters.
   </details>

6. A campaign has working conversion tracking and order values that vary a lot. Which bid
   strategy fits?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Maximize conversion value, adding a target ROAS once volume is steady.
   </details>

7. What's the most Google can spend in a day on a $50 daily budget, and in a month?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Up to $100 in one day. No more than $50 × 30.4 = $1,520 in a month.
   </details>

8. A profitable keyword loses 40% of impression share to budget. What can you do?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Cut waste elsewhere in the same campaign to free budget, or move the keyword into its own
   campaign with its own budget.
   </details>

9. What's the main risk of Performance Max?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   It can claim credit for brand searches and returning customers who would have bought anyway,
   and it offers less control and visibility.
   </details>

10. Why exclude purchasers from acquisition ads?

    <details><summary>Answer</summary>

    You'd pay to show "buy now" messages to people who already bought, which wastes money and
    annoys customers.
    </details>
