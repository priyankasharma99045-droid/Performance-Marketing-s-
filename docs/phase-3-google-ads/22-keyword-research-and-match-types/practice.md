# Practice 22: Build a keyword list

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Google Sheets, and Keyword Planner if you have a Google Ads account · **Save to:** A sheet named `Keywords` in your Drive course folder. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-22.md`.

## Goal

Build a list of 50 keywords for your practice business, sorted by intent, with a match type for
each.

## Steps

1. Create a sheet with these columns: `keyword`, `intent`, `avg_monthly_searches`,
   `top_of_page_bid_high`, `match_type`, `keep`.
2. Write 10 **seed keywords** from your personas, offer, and competitor notes.
3. Expand the list to at least 50 ideas:
   - **With Keyword Planner:** Go to **Tools > Keyword Planner > Discover new keywords**.
     Enter your seeds. Set your country and language. Copy ideas with their search ranges and
     top-of-page bids.
   - **Without Keyword Planner:** Type each seed into Google and collect autocomplete
     suggestions and "People also ask" questions. Use [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/)
     to compare a few. Leave the volume columns blank.
4. Label each keyword's intent: informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional.
5. Set `keep` to `no` for keywords that don't fit your business or are informational. Add the
   reason in a note. Some of these become negatives in lesson 23.
6. For each kept keyword, choose phrase or exact match. Use exact for your most valuable,
   specific terms.
7. Highlight your 5 best keywords and write one sentence on why for each.

## Done when

- [ ] The sheet has at least 50 keywords, each with an intent label.
- [ ] Unsuitable keywords are marked `no` with a reason.
- [ ] Every kept keyword has a match type.
- [ ] Your top 5 are highlighted with reasons.

## Check yourself

1. Classify the intent: "pebble coffee login", "best espresso beans for home", "how long do coffee
   beans last", "buy dark roast coffee beans".

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Navigational, commercial, informational, transactional.
   </details>

2. Your phrase match keyword is "coffee gift". Could it show for "coffee gift set for him"?
   For "gift ideas"?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Yes for "coffee gift set for him": it includes the meaning of "coffee gift". No for "gift
   ideas": it doesn't include the meaning of coffee.
   </details>

3. Why is broad match risky in a brand-new account with no conversion tracking?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Broad match depends on Google's signals and Smart Bidding to find the right searches. With
   no conversion data, Google can't tell good matches from bad ones, so ads show for loosely
   related searches that waste money.
   </details>

4. Why can long-tail keywords be more profitable than head terms?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Their intent is clearer, so they convert better, and there's usually less competition, so
   clicks cost less.
   </details>
