# Lesson 24: Responsive search ads and assets

**Phase 3 · Week 5 · Day 4** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

The ad is the only thing a searcher sees before deciding whether to click. Its text affects CTR,
Quality Score, CPC, and who clicks. Assets make the ad bigger and more useful, which also lifts
Ad Rank. This is where your copywriting from lesson 7 meets the auction.

## Key ideas

### How a responsive search ad works

A **responsive search ad (RSA)** isn't one fixed ad. You supply parts, and Google mixes them:

| Part | Minimum | Maximum | Character limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines | 3 | 15 | 30 each |
| Descriptions | 2 | 4 | 90 each |
| Display path | 0 | 2 fields | 15 each |
| Final URL | 1 | 1 | |

Google shows up to 3 headlines and 2 descriptions at a time, choosing the combination it
predicts works best for each search. So **every headline must make sense on its own and next
to any other headline.**

The **display path** is decoration on the shown URL, such as `pebble.example/coffee/fresh`. It
doesn't need to be a real page.

### Writing 15 headlines

Give Google real variety. A useful mix:

| Type | How many | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword-focused | 3–4 | Fresh Roasted Coffee Beans |
| Benefit | 3–4 | Cafe-Quality Coffee at Home |
| Offer | 2 | 20% Off Your First Bag |
| Proof | 2 | Rated 4.8 by 2,000 Customers |
| Objection | 2 | Pause or Cancel in One Click |
| Call to action | 1–2 | Start Your Subscription Today |

Avoid near-duplicates. "Fresh Coffee Beans" and "Fresh Beans of Coffee" waste two slots.

### Ad strength

Google rates each RSA's **ad strength** from Poor to Excellent, based on variety and keyword
use. It's guidance for writing, not a performance result. A "Good" ad with a strong offer can
beat an "Excellent" one with a weak offer.

### Pinning

**Pinning** fixes a headline or description to a position, such as "always show the brand name
in headline 1". Content pinned to headline position 1 or 2, or description position 1, always
shows. Pinning reduces the combinations Google can test, so pin only when you must, such as
for a required legal statement.

### Assets

Assets, formerly called extensions, add extra information to the ad:

| Asset | What it adds | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| **Sitelinks** | Extra links to specific pages | 25-character link text, two 35-character description lines |
| **Callouts** | Short benefit phrases | 25 characters each |
| **Structured snippets** | A header with a list, such as "Types: Light, Medium, Dark" | 25 characters per value |
| **Call** | A phone number | |
| **Image** | A picture next to the ad | |
| **Price** and **Promotion** | Prices or a sale | |

Assets take up more space on the page, which usually raises CTR. Google also counts their
expected impact in Ad Rank. Add at least 4 sitelinks, 4 callouts, and 1 structured snippet to
every campaign.

### Automation

Google can generate extra headlines and descriptions from your landing page, especially when
AI Max text customization is on. Review what it writes. It can produce claims you can't back
up.

## Common mistakes

- **Headlines that only make sense in order:** "Our Coffee Is" + "The Freshest" breaks when
  mixed.
- **Too much pinning:** Turns a flexible ad into a fixed one.
- **No assets:** Smaller ad, lower CTR, lower Ad Rank.
