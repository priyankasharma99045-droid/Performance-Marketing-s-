# Practice 24: Write a full responsive search ad

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Your `Structure` tab from practice 23 · **Save to:** A new tab named `Ads` in your keyword sheet. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-24.md`.

## Goal

Write a complete RSA and a full set of assets for your most important ad group.

## Steps

1. Pick your most important non-brand ad group.
2. Create a tab named `Ads` with three columns: `type`, `text`, `length`. In the `length`
   column, use `=LEN(B2)` and copy it down.
3. Add conditional formatting to `length`: red when over 30 for headlines, 90 for descriptions,
   or 25 for callouts and sitelink text.
4. Write **15 headlines** using the mix from the lesson. Include your ad group's main keyword in
   at least 3.
5. Write **4 descriptions**. Each one must include a benefit and a call to action.
6. Write **2 display path** fields, each up to 15 characters.
7. Write **4 sitelinks**, each with link text and two description lines.
8. Write **4 callouts**.
9. Write **1 structured snippet**: choose a header, such as Types, Brands, or Services, and 3–4
   values.
10. Read three random combinations of 3 headlines and 2 descriptions together. Fix any
    combination that sounds odd or repeats itself.

## Done when

- [ ] 15 headlines and 4 descriptions are written, all within limits.
- [ ] At least 3 headlines include the main keyword.
- [ ] 4 sitelinks, 4 callouts, and 1 structured snippet are written.
- [ ] You tested three random combinations and fixed problems.

## Check yourself

1. Why must every RSA headline work on its own?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Google picks and orders headlines itself. Any headline can appear next to any other, so
   each must make sense alone and in any combination.
   </details>

2. Ad strength says "Excellent", but CTR and conversions are worse than an older "Good" ad.
   Which do you trust?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   The results. Ad strength rates how the ad is built, not how it performs.
   </details>

3. When does pinning make sense?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   When a message must always appear in a set position, such as a legal disclaimer or required
   wording. Otherwise, avoid it so Google can test combinations.
   </details>

4. Name two ways assets improve results.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   They make the ad bigger and more useful, which usually raises CTR. Their expected impact is
   also part of Ad Rank, which can improve position or lower CPC.
   </details>
