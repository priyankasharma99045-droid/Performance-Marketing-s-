# Lesson 27: Search terms and reports

**Phase 3 · Week 6 · Day 2** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

Your keywords are what you bid on. **Search terms** are what people actually typed. The gap
between them is where money disappears. Reviewing search terms every week is the single most
valuable routine task in Google Ads.

## Key ideas

### Keywords compared with search terms

| | Keyword | Search term |
|---|---|---|
| **Who writes it** | You | The searcher |
| **Example** | "coffee beans" (phrase) | "free coffee beans samples" |
| **Where to see it** | Keywords report | Search terms report |

Google shows search terms with enough volume. Some low-volume terms are hidden for privacy.

### The weekly search terms routine

1. Open **Insights and reports > Search terms** (menu names vary). Set the date range to the
   last 7 or 30 days.
2. Sort by **cost**, highest first.
3. For each term, decide:

| What you see | Action |
|---|---|
| Irrelevant, with cost and no conversions | Add as a **negative** |
| Relevant, converts well, not yet a keyword | Add as a **keyword**, usually exact or phrase |
| Your brand name in a non-brand campaign | Add your brand as a negative there, so the brand campaign handles it |
| Relevant but too little data | Leave it and check again next week |

### Reading any report well

- **Start with spend:** Big-spend rows matter most. A $3 problem isn't worth your time.
- **Compare with your target:** CPA or ROAS against break-even, not against gut feeling.
- **Watch sample size:** 1 conversion from 5 clicks isn't a 20% conversion rate you can rely
  on. Lesson 34 explains why.
- **Segment:** Split by device, day of week, or network to find hidden patterns.
- **Compare periods:** This week against last week, or this month against the same month last
  year.

### Impression share

Impression share tells you how much of the possible traffic you're getting:

| Metric | Meaning | If high |
|---|---|---|
| **Search impression share** | Share of eligible searches where you showed | |
| **Search lost IS (budget)** | Missed because the budget ran out | Raise the budget, if the keyword is profitable |
| **Search lost IS (rank)** | Missed because Ad Rank was too low | Improve quality, or raise bids |

### Turning findings into a recommendation

Use the same format as lesson 14:

- **What:** "7 search terms with no conversions cost $454.75 last month."
- **Why:** "Phrase match on 'coffee beans' matched informational and job searches."
- **So what:** "Add 7 negatives. Expected saving: about $450 a month."

## Common mistakes

- **Never checking search terms:** Waste builds up silently for months.
- **Adding negatives from tiny data:** One expensive click isn't a pattern.
- **Excluding brand terms everywhere:** Exclude them from non-brand campaigns only.
