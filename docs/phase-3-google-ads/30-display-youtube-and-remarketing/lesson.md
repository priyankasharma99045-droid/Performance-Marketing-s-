# Lesson 30: Display, YouTube, and remarketing

**Phase 3 · Week 6 · Day 5** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

Search only reaches people who are already searching. Google's visual channels (Display,
YouTube, and Demand Gen) reach people earlier in the funnel, and bring back people who visited
but didn't buy. They work more like social ads than search ads, which prepares you for Meta in
phase 4.

## Key ideas

### Google's visual channels

| Channel | Where ads show | Typical role |
|---|---|---|
| **Display Network** | Millions of partner websites and apps | Cheap reach and remarketing. Low intent. |
| **YouTube** | Before, during, and beside videos, and in Shorts | Demonstrating and explaining products |
| **Demand Gen** | YouTube (including Shorts), Discover, Gmail, and more | Social-style image and video ads aimed at conversions |

### YouTube ad formats

| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Skippable in-stream** | Plays before or during a video. Viewers can skip after 5 seconds. |
| **Non-skippable in-stream** | Short ads that can't be skipped |
| **Bumper** | 6 seconds, non-skippable |
| **In-feed** | Shown in YouTube search results and next to videos |
| **Shorts** | Vertical ads between Shorts |

Because people can skip, **the first 5 seconds decide everything**.

### The ABCD framework

Google's research-based guide for effective video ads:

- **Attract:** Hook attention from the first second. Fast start, faces, movement, a question.
- **Brand:** Show the brand early and often, not only at the end.
- **Connect:** Make people think or feel something. Show the product in use, tell a mini story.
- **Direct:** Ask for a specific action: "Get 20% off your first bag".

### Audiences

On visual channels, you choose **who** sees the ad, not which search they typed:

| Audience type | Built from | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Your data** | Your site visitors, app users, YouTube viewers, and customer lists (Customer Match) | People who added to cart in the last 7 days |
| **Custom segments** | Search terms, websites, or apps that describe your audience | People who searched "coffee subscription" |
| **In-market** | Google's view of people actively shopping in a category | In-market for coffee |
| **Affinity** | Long-term interests | Food lovers |
| **Demographics and life events** | Age, parental status, moving home | |

### Remarketing

**Remarketing** shows ads to people who already visited or engaged. It's usually the
cheapest conversion source outside brand search, because these people already know you.

Build segments by how close people came to converting:

| Segment | Duration | Message |
|---|---|---|
| All visitors | 30 days | Remind and show the main benefit |
| Product viewers | 14 days | Show the product they viewed, plus reviews |
| Cart abandoners | 7 days | Remove the last doubt: free shipping, guarantee |
| Purchasers | 180 days | **Exclude** from acquisition ads. Show a reorder or upsell message. |

**Membership duration** is how long someone stays in a segment after qualifying. Google allows
up to 540 days.

### Risks

- **Over-frequency:** Showing the same ad 20 times annoys people. Watch frequency.
- **Taking credit for sales that would happen anyway:** Cart abandoners often come back on their
  own. Remarketing ROAS looks great partly for that reason. Lesson 40.
- **Low-quality Display placements:** Check where ads appear and exclude poor sites and apps.

## Common mistakes

- **Not excluding purchasers:** Paying to show "Buy now" to people who recently bought.
- **Video ads that save the brand for the end:** Most viewers skip before they see it.
- **One remarketing audience for everyone:** A homepage visitor and a cart abandoner need
  different messages.
