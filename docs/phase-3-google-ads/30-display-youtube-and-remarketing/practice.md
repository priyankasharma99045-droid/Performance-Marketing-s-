# Practice 30: Plan remarketing and script a video ad

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Your marketing brief · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/lesson-30.md`

## Goal

Design a remarketing audience plan for your practice business, and script a short video ad with
the ABCD framework.

## Steps

### Part 1: Remarketing plan (15 minutes)

1. Fill in this table for at least 5 segments. Base them on your funnel from lesson 10:

   | Segment | Rule (who's in it) | Duration | Message | Exclude |
   |---|---|---|---|---|

2. Make sure purchasers or converted leads are excluded from acquisition ads, and have their own
   message.
3. Write one custom segment: 5–10 search terms your ideal customers type.
4. Note the event or page each segment depends on. Is it tracked in your tracking plan? If not,
   add it to the plan's **Known gaps** section.

### Part 2: A 15-second video script (15 minutes)

Write a script for a 15-second YouTube or Demand Gen video, second by second:

| Seconds | What's on screen | What's said or shown as text | ABCD letter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | | | Attract |
| 2–5 | | | Brand |
| 5–12 | | | Connect |
| 12–15 | | | Direct |

Rules:

- The brand appears within the first 5 seconds.
- The first 2 seconds make sense with the sound off.
- The call to action is specific.

## Done when

- [ ] At least 5 remarketing segments are planned, with rules, durations, messages, and
      exclusions.
- [ ] Purchasers or converted leads are excluded from acquisition ads.
- [ ] Each segment's tracking need is checked against your tracking plan.
- [ ] The 15-second script is complete, with the brand in the first 5 seconds.

## Check yourself

1. Why do cart abandoners get a shorter membership duration than all visitors?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Their intent is high but fades fast. After about a week, most have bought elsewhere or lost
   interest. A short window keeps the message timely and the cost focused.
   </details>

2. Remarketing shows a ROAS of 9. Why might that overstate its real value?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Many of these people would have come back and bought anyway. The ads take credit for some
   sales they didn't cause.
   </details>

3. What do the letters in ABCD stand for?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Attract, Brand, Connect, Direct.
   </details>

4. Why show the brand early in a skippable video ad?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Many viewers skip after 5 seconds. If the brand appears only at the end, most people never
   see it.
   </details>
