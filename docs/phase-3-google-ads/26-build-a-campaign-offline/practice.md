# Practice 26: Build your Search campaign without publishing it

**30–45 minutes** · **You need:** Your sheets from practices 22–25, and Google Ads Editor if you have an account · **Save to:** A sheet named `Campaign build` in your Drive course folder. Put the link and checklist in `my-work/notes/lesson-26.md`.

## Goal

Build one complete, launch-ready Search campaign, either in Ads Editor or in the import
spreadsheet, without spending anything.

## Steps

### Part 1: Fill in the build sheet (25 minutes)

1. Import [`practice-kit/templates/search-campaign-build.csv`](../../../practice-kit/templates/search-campaign-build.csv)
   into a new Google Sheet. It contains example rows for Pebble.
2. Replace the example rows with your own non-brand campaign:
   - **1 campaign row:** name from your naming standard, type `Search`, budget from practice 25,
     and bid strategy.
   - **1 row per ad group.**
   - **1 row per keyword**, with the match type in `Criterion Type`: `Exact`, `Phrase`, or
     `Broad`.
   - **Negative rows**, with `Criterion Type` set to `Negative Phrase`, `Negative Exact`, or
     `Negative Broad`.
   - **1 RSA row per ad group** with all headlines, descriptions, final URL, and paths.
3. Use your landing page URL from lesson 15 as the final URL.

### Part 2: Load it into Ads Editor (optional, 10 minutes)

If you created a Google Ads account in lesson 21:

1. Install [Google Ads Editor](https://business.google.com/en-all/ad-tools/google-ads-editor/)
   and sign in. Download your account.
2. Use the import option (often under **Account > Import**, or **Make multiple changes**) to
   add your sheet's rows. Download it as CSV first.
3. Review the proposed changes and fix any errors Ads Editor flags.
4. **Don't select Post.** Your campaign stays on your computer only. Screenshot the campaign in
   Ads Editor.

If an import column name isn't recognized, export a sample from Ads Editor and match its
headers.

### Part 3: Run the checklist (5 minutes)

Copy the pre-launch checklist from the lesson into your notes. Tick each item that your build
meets. For each one you can't tick, write what's missing.

## Done when

- [ ] The build sheet has a full campaign: ad groups, keywords, negatives, and one RSA per ad
      group.
- [ ] Every headline and description is within its character limit.
- [ ] The pre-launch checklist is completed, with gaps explained.
- [ ] Optional: the campaign appears in Ads Editor and was **not** posted.

## Check yourself

1. You make 200 keyword changes in Ads Editor. When do they affect the live account?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Only when you select **Post**. Until then, they exist only on your computer.
   </details>

2. Pebble targets the United States with the default location option. Why might it pay for
   clicks from people in other countries?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   The default can include people who show **interest** in the location, such as someone
   abroad searching about US coffee brands. Choosing **Presence** limits ads to people in, or
   regularly in, the area.
   </details>

3. Why turn off the Display Network in a Search campaign?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Display placements show image and text ads on websites, where people aren't searching. They
   bring cheap, low-intent clicks that mix with and distort search results.
   </details>
