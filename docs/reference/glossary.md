# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used in this course. Formulas live in the
[metrics cheat sheet](metrics-cheatsheet.md). The number in brackets is the lesson that
teaches the term.

## A

- **A/B test:** Showing two versions to similar groups at the same time to see which performs better. [34]
- **Ad group:** In Google Ads, a container inside a campaign that holds related keywords and ads. [23]
- **Ad Rank:** Google's score that decides whether your ad shows and in what position. It uses your bid, ad quality, and context. [21]
- **Ad set:** In Meta, the level below a campaign where you choose audience, placements, budget (sometimes), and schedule. [31]
- **Advantage+:** Meta's family of automated options, such as Advantage+ audience, placements, and campaign budget. [32]
- **AI Max for Search:** Google's optional layer on Search campaigns that expands matching to more searches and rewrites ad text automatically. [22]
- **AIDA:** Attention, Interest, Desire, Action. A simple model of how people move toward buying. [2]
- **Asset:** In Google Ads, an extra piece of an ad, such as a sitelink, callout, image, or phone number. Formerly called extensions. [24]
- **Asset group:** In Performance Max, a bundle of headlines, images, videos, and audience signals for one theme. [29]
- **Attribution:** The rules for deciding which ad or channel gets credit for a conversion. [20]
- **Attribution window:** How long after a click or view a conversion can still be credited to an ad. Also called lookback window. [20]
- **Auction:** The instant process that decides which ads show each time someone searches or scrolls. [21]
- **Audience signal:** A hint you give Performance Max about who your customers are. Google can go beyond it. [29]
- **AOV (average order value):** Average revenue per order. [4]

## B

- **Bid strategy:** How the platform sets bids, such as Manual CPC, Maximize conversions, or Target ROAS. [25]
- **Blended ROAS:** See MER.
- **Brand campaign:** A Search campaign that targets searches for your own brand name. [23]
- **Break-even ROAS:** The ROAS at which ad spend equals gross profit. 1 ÷ margin. [4]
- **Broad match:** A keyword match type that can show your ad for searches related to the keyword's meaning. [22]

## C

- **CAC (customer acquisition cost):** Total marketing cost to win one new customer. [4]
- **Callout:** A short Google Ads asset, such as "Free shipping". [24]
- **Campaign:** The top level of an ad account. Sets the goal and, often, the budget. [21, 31]
- **CAPI (Conversions API):** Meta's way to send conversion events from your server instead of the browser. [18]
- **Catalog:** A product list uploaded to Meta for dynamic product ads. [32]
- **Consent Mode:** Google's system for adjusting tags based on whether a visitor accepted cookies. [19]
- **Conversion:** A valuable action you track, such as a purchase or a lead form submission. [3]
- **Cookie:** A small file a website stores in the browser to remember the visitor. [11]
- **CPA, CPC, CPM, CTR, CVR:** See the [metrics cheat sheet](metrics-cheatsheet.md). [3]
- **Creative:** The visible part of an ad: image, video, and text. [33]
- **Creative fatigue:** When an ad's results drop because the same people have seen it too often. [35]
- **CRM (customer relationship management):** Software that stores leads and customers and tracks their status. [37]
- **CRO (conversion rate optimization):** Improving a page or flow so more visitors convert. [9]
- **Custom audience:** In Meta, a group built from your own data, such as site visitors or a customer list. [32]

## D

- **Data-driven attribution (DDA):** An attribution model that uses your account's data to share credit across touchpoints. [20]
- **DataLayer:** A JavaScript list on a web page that passes information to Google Tag Manager. [16]
- **Data Studio:** Google's free dashboard tool. Called Looker Studio from 2022 to 2026. [39]
- **DebugView:** The GA4 screen that shows events from your own test visits in real time. [17]
- **Demand Gen:** A Google campaign type for visual ads on YouTube, Discover, and Gmail. [30]
- **Display Network:** Google's network of partner websites and apps that show image ads. [30]

## E

- **Engaged session:** In GA4, a session that lasted over 10 seconds, had a key event, or had 2 or more page views. [13]
- **Enhanced conversions:** Sending hashed customer data, such as email, with Google Ads conversions to improve matching. [18]
- **Event:** In GA4, any tracked interaction, such as `page_view` or `purchase`. [13]
- **Exact match:** A keyword match type that shows your ad only for searches with the same meaning as the keyword. [22]

## F

- **First-party data:** Data your business collects directly from customers. [11]
- **Frequency:** Average number of times one person saw your ad. [35]
- **Funnel:** The stages people pass through, from first hearing of you to buying. [2]

## G

- **GA4 (Google Analytics 4):** Google's free website and app analytics tool. [13]
- **GTM (Google Tag Manager):** A free tool for adding and managing tracking tags without editing site code each time. [16]
- **Gross margin:** The share of revenue left after the cost of the product. [4]

## H

- **Hook:** The first 1 to 3 seconds of a video ad, or the first line of copy. Its job is to stop the scroll. [33]
- **Hook rate:** See the [metrics cheat sheet](metrics-cheatsheet.md). [35]

## I

- **Impression:** One showing of an ad. [3]
- **Impression share:** The share of eligible auctions where your ad showed. [27]
- **Incrementality:** The conversions that happened only because of the ad, and wouldn't have happened anyway. [40]

## K

- **Key event:** A GA4 event you mark as important to the business. Called a conversion in GA4 before 2024. [17]
- **Keyword:** A word or phrase you bid on in Search ads. [22]
- **KPI (key performance indicator):** The main number you judge success by. [3]

## L

- **Landing page:** The page someone lands on after clicking an ad. [9]
- **Lead:** A potential customer who shared contact details. [2]
- **Learning phase:** The period after launch or a big edit when Meta or Google is still learning who to show ads to. Results swing more. [25, 35]
- **Lookalike audience:** In Meta, people similar to a source audience you provide. [32]
- **LTV (lifetime value):** The total gross profit one customer brings over time. [4]

## M

- **Match type:** The setting that controls how closely a search must match your keyword. [22]
- **Media plan:** A document that says where the budget goes, why, and what results to expect. [38]
- **MER (marketing efficiency ratio):** Total revenue ÷ total ad spend, across all channels. [38]
- **Merchant Center:** Google's tool for uploading a product feed used by Shopping ads. [29]
- **MMM (marketing mix modeling):** A statistical method that estimates each channel's impact from aggregated data over time. [40]

## N

- **Negative keyword:** A word that stops your ad from showing for searches that contain it. [23]

## O

- **Objective:** The goal you choose when you create a campaign, such as Sales or Leads. Platforms optimize toward it. [31]
- **Offline conversion import:** Uploading conversions that happen outside the website, such as a closed sale in a CRM. [18]

## P

- **Performance Max (PMax):** A Google campaign type that runs across all Google inventory from one campaign. [29]
- **Persona:** A short profile of a typical customer: needs, worries, and how they decide. [6]
- **Phrase match:** A keyword match type that shows your ad for searches that include the meaning of the keyword. [22]
- **Pixel:** A snippet of code that reports site visits and actions back to an ad platform. [11, 18]
- **Placement:** Where an ad appears, such as Instagram Stories or the Facebook feed. [31]
- **Prospecting:** Showing ads to people who don't know you yet. [36]

## Q

- **Quality Score:** Google's 1–10 diagnostic for a keyword, based on expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience. [21]

## R

- **Reach:** The number of unique people who saw an ad. [3]
- **Remarketing:** Google's term for retargeting. [30]
- **Retargeting:** Showing ads to people who already visited or engaged. [36]
- **RSA (responsive search ad):** A Google Search ad where you supply up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions, and Google mixes them. [24]

## S

- **Search term:** The exact words someone typed. Different from your keyword. [27]
- **Session:** In GA4, a group of interactions by one user within a time frame. [13]
- **Sitelink:** A Google Ads asset that adds extra links below the ad. [24]
- **Smart Bidding:** Google's automated bid strategies that optimize for conversions or conversion value. [25]
- **Statistical significance:** A measure of how unlikely a test result is to be random chance. [34]

## T

- **Tag:** A snippet of code that sends data to a tool such as GA4 or Meta. [11]
- **Target CPA (tCPA), target ROAS (tROAS):** Goals you give Smart Bidding. [25]
- **Trigger:** In GTM, the rule that decides when a tag fires. [16]

## U

- **Unit economics:** The money made or lost on one order or one customer. [4]
- **USP (unique selling point):** What makes your offer better or different. [7]
- **UTM parameters:** Tags added to a link so GA4 knows where the visit came from. [12]

## V

- **Variable:** In GTM, a value that tags and triggers can read, such as the page URL. [16]
- **View-through conversion:** A conversion after someone saw an ad but didn't click it. [20]
