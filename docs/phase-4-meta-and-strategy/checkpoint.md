## Part 3: Final review (30 minutes)

Answer from memory in `my-work/notes/week-08-review.md`, then check.

1. Explain performance marketing, and how you'd judge whether it's working, in three sentences.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   It's advertising measured against countable results, such as sales or leads, with money
   moved toward what works. You judge it by comparing CPA or ROAS with break-even targets from
   the business's margins. At the business level, you check MER and incrementality, not only
   platform-reported results.
   </details>

2. Which Meta objective and optimization event fit an online store?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   The Sales objective, optimizing for the Purchase event.
   </details>

3. What three signs together show creative fatigue?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Frequency rising, CTR falling, and cost per result rising.
   </details>

4. An ad set says "Learning limited". Name two fixes.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Any two of: combine ad sets, widen the audience, raise the budget, or optimize for a more
   frequent event.
   </details>

5. Test A converts 50 of 1,000 visitors, and test B 60 of 1,000. Can you call B the winner?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   No. The difference is too small for the sample size. The p-value is about 0.33, well above
   0.05. Keep testing, or test a bigger change.
   </details>

6. Why does prospecting exclude recent site visitors?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   They belong in retargeting with a different message, and including them makes prospecting
   look better than it is.
   </details>

7. What makes LinkedIn worth its high CPCs for some businesses?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Precise B2B targeting by job title and company, for businesses where one customer is worth a
   lot.
   </details>

8. What's the difference between attribution and incrementality?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Attribution assigns credit for conversions to touchpoints. Incrementality measures which
   conversions happened only because of the ads.
   </details>

9. Why does CPA usually rise as you scale budget?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Diminishing returns. The extra spend reaches people who are less likely to convert, so the
   marginal CPA rises.
   </details>

10. Look back at your week 1 notes. What do you understand now that you didn't then?

    <details><summary>Answer</summary>

    No fixed answer. Write it down. It's the clearest measure of how far you've come.
    </details>
