# Lesson 37: Other channels and email

**Phase 4 · Week 8 · Day 2** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

Google and Meta take most performance budgets, but not all. Business-to-business (B2B) companies
live on LinkedIn. Younger audiences live on TikTok. Microsoft Advertising often brings cheaper
search clicks. Email turns one-time buyers into repeat customers for almost no cost. Knowing
where each channel fits lets you recommend the right mix, not only the familiar one.

## Key ideas

### LinkedIn Ads

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Targeting by job title, seniority, company, industry, and company size |
| **Best for** | B2B: software, professional services, recruiting, high-value leads |
| **Formats** | Sponsored content (feed posts), document ads, video, message ads, **Lead Gen Forms** |
| **Watch out** | CPCs are usually far higher than on Meta. It only pays off when a customer is worth a lot. |

### TikTok Ads

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Huge reach with younger audiences. Entertainment-first viewing. |
| **Best for** | Consumer products that are quick to demonstrate in short, native-style video |
| **Formats** | In-feed video. **Spark Ads**, which promote existing organic or creator posts. |
| **Watch out** | Ads that look like ads fail. Creative must feel like TikTok content. Needs a constant flow of new videos. |
| **Tracking** | TikTok Pixel and Events API, similar to Meta's Pixel and Conversions API |

### Microsoft Advertising

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Search ads on Bing and partner sites. Often lower CPCs than Google, with less competition. |
| **Best for** | Extending a working Google Search campaign. Audiences that skew older, desktop, or B2B. |
| **Shortcut** | Import your Google Ads campaigns directly. |
| **Watch out** | Much lower volume than Google |

### Other channels to know about

- **Pinterest:** Visual discovery and planning, such as home, fashion, food, and weddings.
- **Amazon Ads:** Retail media. Ads for products sold on Amazon.
- **Reddit:** Niche communities. Message must respect the community's culture.
- **Snapchat:** Younger audiences, vertical video.

### Email: the owned channel

Email costs almost nothing per message, and you own the list. No algorithm decides who sees it.

**Automated flows** do most of the work:

| Flow | Trigger | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome** | New subscriber | Introduce the brand, deliver any promised discount |
| **Abandoned cart** | Cart left without buying | Remind and remove doubts |
| **Browse abandonment** | Viewed products, didn't add to cart | Show what they looked at |
| **Post-purchase** | After an order | Thank, explain use, ask for a review |
| **Win-back** | No purchase in a long time | Bring lapsed customers back |

**Email metrics:**

| Metric | Note |
|---|---|
| Open rate | Unreliable since 2021. Apple Mail Privacy Protection can mark emails as opened automatically. |
| Click rate | Clicks ÷ delivered. A better signal of interest. |
| Conversion rate, revenue per recipient | The metrics that matter |
| Unsubscribe and spam complaint rate | Keep low. High rates hurt delivery. |

**Deliverability:** Large mailbox providers such as Gmail and Yahoo require bulk senders to
authenticate their domain (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC), offer one-click unsubscribe, and keep spam
complaints low. Without that, emails land in spam.

### CRM

A CRM tracks leads and customers through stages:

```
New lead → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won or Lost
```

For lead generation, the CRM is where you learn lead quality, and where offline conversions
(lesson 18) come from.

## Common mistakes

- **LinkedIn for a $20 product:** The CPCs can't pay back.
- **Repurposing polished TV ads on TikTok:** They look out of place and get skipped.
- **Judging email on open rate:** Inflated by privacy features.
- **No welcome or cart flows:** The cheapest revenue a store can get, left unused.
