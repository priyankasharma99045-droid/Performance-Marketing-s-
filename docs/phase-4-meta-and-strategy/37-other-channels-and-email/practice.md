business.

## Check yourself

1. Where would you start for the B2B software company, and why?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   A strong answer: Google Search for category and problem searches, plus LinkedIn for targeting
   by job title and company. A customer is worth thousands, so LinkedIn's high CPCs can pay back.
   TikTok is a poor fit.
   </details>

2. Where would you start for the local dentist?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Google Search, for urgent local searches such as "dentist near me". LinkedIn is a poor fit:
   wrong intent and too expensive.
   </details>

3. Why isn't open rate a reliable email metric?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Apple Mail Privacy Protection can load emails automatically, so they count as opened even if
   nobody read them. Use clicks, conversions, and revenue per recipient.
   </details>

4. What's the main creative rule for TikTok?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Make ads that feel like native TikTok content: vertical, fast, often creator-led. Polished,
   ad-looking videos get skipped.
   </details>
