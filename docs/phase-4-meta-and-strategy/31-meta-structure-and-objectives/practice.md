# Practice 31: Plan and draft a Meta campaign

**30 minutes** · **You need:** A Facebook account (optional), Google Sheets · **Save to:** A sheet named `Meta plan` in your Drive course folder. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-31.md`.

## Goal

Plan a Meta sales campaign for your practice business, and optionally save it as an unpublished
draft in Ads Manager.

## Steps

### Part 1: Plan the structure (15 minutes)

1. In a sheet, plan one campaign with this structure:

   | Level | Name (your naming standard) | Key settings |
   |---|---|---|
   | Campaign | | Objective, budget type and amount |
   | Ad set 1: prospecting | | Audience, placements, optimization event |
   | Ad set 2: retargeting | | Audience, placements, optimization event |
   | Ads (2–3 per ad set) | | Format, main message |

2. Choose the objective and optimization event. Write one sentence on why.
3. Choose between Advantage+ campaign budget and ad set budgets. Write one sentence on why.
4. Write the UTM parameters for the ads, using your UTM sheet from practice 12. Use
   `{{campaign.name}}` and `{{ad.name}}` where useful.

### Part 2: Build a draft (optional, 15 minutes)

You need a Facebook account. Don't add a payment method.

1. Create a business portfolio, following
   [Create a business portfolio](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1710077379203657).
2. Open Ads Manager and select **Create**. Choose your objective.
3. Build the campaign, one ad set, and one ad from your plan. Use any image you own.
4. Close without publishing. Ads Manager keeps it as a draft. Screenshot the campaign, ad set,
   and ad settings.

If you skip Part 2, spend the time writing down each setting you'd choose on every screen,
using the help pages linked in the lesson.

## Done when

- [ ] The sheet shows a campaign, two ad sets, and 2–3 ads per ad set.
- [ ] The objective, optimization event, and budget choice each have a reason.
- [ ] UTM parameters are written.
- [ ] Optional: a draft exists in Ads Manager and was **not** published.

## Check yourself

1. A store selects the Traffic objective because clicks are cheap. What's the likely result?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Lots of cheap clicks from people who click often but rarely buy. Meta optimized for exactly
   what was asked: clicks.
   </details>

2. At which level do you choose the audience?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   The ad set.
   </details>

3. What two things, besides your bid, decide Meta's auction?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   The estimated action rate, meaning how likely the person is to take your optimization
   action, and ad quality.
   </details>
