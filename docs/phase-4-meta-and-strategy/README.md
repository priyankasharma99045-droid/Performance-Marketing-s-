# Phase 4: Meta, other channels, and strategy

**Weeks 7–8** · 10 lessons, 1 weekly review, 1 checkpoint

## What you'll be able to do

- Plan Meta campaigns with the right objective, structure, and audiences.
- Write and test ad creative, and judge test results with basic statistics.
- Diagnose a Meta account: learning phase, fatigue, and weak creative.
- Design a full-funnel setup, and know where LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft Ads, and email fit.
- Build a media plan with a forecast, and report results clearly.
- Audit an account, scale carefully, and measure incrementality.

## Before you start

Lesson 31 has an optional step that needs a Facebook account. You don't need to add a payment
method. Every practice works without one.

## Week 7 review

**Saturday** · 30–45 minutes · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/week-07-review.md`, using
[`templates/weekly-review.md`](../../templates/weekly-review.md)

Answer from memory first, then check against the lessons.

1. Draw Meta's campaign, ad set, and ad structure. What's decided at each level?
2. Why does the Traffic objective rarely suit a sales goal?
3. Explain "the creative is the targeting" in your own words.
4. Which of your three concepts from practice 33 would you test first, and why?
5. Write the formulas for hook rate and hold rate. What does each one diagnose?
6. Why is peeking at a test every day a problem?
7. What does "Learning limited" mean, and what causes it?

Then catch up on any unfinished practice from the week.
