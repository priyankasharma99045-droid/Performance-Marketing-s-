# Lesson 36: Retargeting and full funnel

**Phase 4 · Week 8 · Day 1** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

Few people buy the first time they see an ad. A full-funnel setup meets people at each stage with
the right message, without wasting money showing the same thing to everyone. It's also where you
learn to balance cheap-looking retargeting results against the prospecting that feeds them.

## Key ideas

### The three stages on Meta

| Stage | Audience | Message | Optimize for | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prospecting** | Broad or lookalike. Excludes visitors and customers. | Problem, benefit, story | Purchase | CPA, new customers |
| **Retargeting** | Visitors, engagers, cart abandoners. Excludes recent buyers. | Proof, offer, objection handling | Purchase | CPA, ROAS |
| **Retention** | Past customers | Reorder, upsell, referral | Purchase | Repeat purchase rate, LTV |

### Exclusions keep stages clean

Each stage excludes the people that belong to a later one:

```
Prospecting   excludes  site visitors (30 days) and purchasers
Retargeting   excludes  purchasers (last 30 days)
Retention     includes  purchasers only
```

Without exclusions, prospecting budget gets spent on people who already know you, and its
results look better than they are.

### Budget split

Retargeting audiences are small, and they only grow when prospecting brings in new visitors. A
common starting split for a new brand:

| Stage | Share of budget |
|---|---|
| Prospecting | 70–80% |
| Retargeting | 10–20% |
| Retention | 5–10% |

Adjust with data. If retargeting frequency climbs above 5–6 in a week, it has more budget than
its audience can absorb.

### Retargeting looks better than it is

Retargeting usually shows the best ROAS, but many of those people would have bought anyway.
Cutting prospecting to fund more retargeting is a classic mistake: retargeting audiences shrink,
and a few weeks later everything declines.

### Automated full funnel

**Advantage+ sales campaigns** run prospecting and retargeting together and let Meta decide the
split. You can set how Meta treats existing customers. This needs less work but gives you less
visibility. Many advertisers run it alongside a separate retargeting setup and compare.

### Sequencing messages

Retargeting works best when the message moves forward:

1. Viewed a product → show reviews and how it works
2. Added to cart → answer objections: shipping, returns, guarantee
3. Still no purchase after 5 days → offer a small incentive, if the margin allows

### Lead generation funnels

For leads, Meta offers **instant forms**: forms that open inside Facebook or Instagram, with
details pre-filled. They produce cheap leads, but often lower quality, because submitting is so
easy. To improve quality, add a question or two, or use the **higher intent** form type, which adds
a review step. Always judge on qualified leads, not form fills.

### Across channels

Funnels cross platforms. Meta prospecting creates demand. Google brand search captures it.
Email retains it. Judge the system as a whole using MER, not each channel alone.

## Common mistakes

- **No exclusions between stages:** Double spending, and misleading results.
- **Over-funding retargeting:** High frequency, fatigue, and paying for sales that would happen
  anyway.
- **Same message at every stage:** A cart abandoner doesn't need the brand story again.
