# Practice 36: Design a full-funnel setup

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Your audiences from practice 32 and concepts from practice 33 · **Save to:** A new tab named `Funnel` in your `Meta plan` sheet. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-36.md`.

## Goal

Design a three-stage Meta funnel for your practice business, with audiences, exclusions,
messages, and a budget split.

## Steps

1. Fill in this table for prospecting, retargeting, and retention:

   | Stage | Audience | Excludes | Message and creative concept | Optimization event | KPI | Budget share |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

2. Check that no person can be in two stages at once. Fix the exclusions if needed.
3. Split a **$3,000 monthly Meta budget** across the stages, and calculate each stage's daily
   budget. Use `÷ 30.4`.
4. Plan a 3-step retargeting sequence: what someone sees after viewing a product, after adding to
   cart, and after 5 days without buying.
5. Your retargeting frequency hits 7 in one week. Write what you'd change.
6. If your practice business collects leads: decide between instant forms and your landing page
   form, and write how you'd protect lead quality.

## Done when

- [ ] All three stages have audiences, exclusions, messages, events, KPIs, and budget shares.
- [ ] Exclusions stop anyone being in two stages.
- [ ] The $3,000 is split, with daily budgets.
- [ ] The retargeting sequence and the frequency fix are written.

## Check yourself

1. With a 75%, 18%, and 7% split of $3,000, what are the daily budgets?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   - Prospecting: $2,250 a month, $74.01 a day
   - Retargeting: $540 a month, $17.76 a day
   - Retention: $210 a month, $6.91 a day
   </details>

2. Why exclude site visitors from prospecting?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   They already know you, so they belong in retargeting with a different message. Including them
   also makes prospecting results look better than they really are.
   </details>

3. A manager wants to move half the prospecting budget into retargeting because its ROAS is
   higher. What do you say?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Retargeting depends on prospecting to fill its audience. Moving the budget raises retargeting
   frequency now and shrinks its audience later. Many retargeting sales would also have happened
   anyway. Test the change carefully, and judge on total results such as MER.
   </details>

4. Why are leads from instant forms often lower quality?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Forms are pre-filled and open inside the app, so submitting takes almost no effort or
   commitment. Some people submit without real intent.
   </details>
