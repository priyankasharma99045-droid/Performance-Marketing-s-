# Practice 32: Plan your Meta audiences

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Your `Meta plan` sheet from practice 31 · **Save to:** A new tab named `Audiences` in that sheet. Put the link in `my-work/notes/lesson-32.md`.

## Goal

Design every audience your practice business needs on Meta, with sources, sizes, and exclusions.

## Steps

1. Create a table with these columns: `audience_name`, `type`, `source_or_rule`, `window_days`,
   `used_in`, `excluded_from`.
2. Plan at least **4 custom audiences**, such as website visitors, product viewers, add to cart,
   purchasers, video viewers, or Instagram engagers.
3. Plan **2 lookalike audiences**. Name the source and the size for each. Explain why that
   source.
4. Plan **1 broad prospecting audience** with Advantage+ audience. List 2–3 audience suggestions
   you'd give Meta.
5. Fill in `used_in` and `excluded_from` for every audience, so prospecting, retargeting, and
   customers don't overlap.
6. Check whether your practice business falls under a special ad category. Write yes or no, and
   what it would change.
7. Your site gets 1,500 visitors a month. Write 2–3 sentences on whether a "product viewers, 7
   days" audience is big enough, and what you'd do if not.

## Done when

- [ ] At least 4 custom, 2 lookalike, and 1 broad audience are planned.
- [ ] Every audience has its usage and exclusions.
- [ ] The special ad category check is done.
- [ ] The audience size question is answered.

## Check yourself

1. Which source makes a better lookalike: all Page likers, or customers who bought twice?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Repeat customers. The source defines what "similar" means, so a source of your most valuable
   customers finds people more likely to become valuable customers.
   </details>

2. You add interests to an Advantage+ audience. Does Meta show ads only to those people?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   No. With Advantage+ audience, your inputs are suggestions. Meta can go beyond them when it
   expects better results.
   </details>

3. What does "the creative is the targeting" mean?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   With broad targeting, the content of the ad attracts the right people. Meta learns who
   engages and converts with each creative and shows it to more people like them.
   </details>

4. Your 7-day add-to-cart audience has 200 people. What problems might you see?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   High frequency, because the same few people see the ad repeatedly, high CPMs, and an ad set
   that can't exit the learning phase. Widen the window or combine it with other warm audiences.
   </details>
