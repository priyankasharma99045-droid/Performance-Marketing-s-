# Setup

Everything in this course is free. You create most accounts on the day a lesson needs them,
so you don't need to do it all on day 1.

## Before lesson 1

1. **Google account:** Use a personal Gmail account. Every Google tool in this course runs on
   it.
2. **Course folder:** In Google Drive, create a folder named `Performance Marketing Course`.
   Save your spreadsheets there.
3. **This repo on your computer:** Clone or download this repo so you can save notes in
   `my-work/`. If you prefer, edit the files directly on GitHub.
4. **A way to take screenshots:** On a Mac, press Shift+Command+4. On Windows, press
   Windows+Shift+S.

## About spending money

**You don't need to spend anything to finish this course.** Every practice uses free tools,
the GA4 demo account, campaigns saved as drafts, and the sample data in
[`practice-kit/data/`](../../practice-kit/README.md).

Never enter card details into Google Ads or Meta unless you have decided to run a live
campaign.

### Optional: run a real campaign

Running real ads teaches things that sample data can't. If you want to try:

- **When:** After you finish phase 3 (Google Ads) or phase 4 (Meta). Not before. You need
  working tracking first.
- **What business:** A real one. A friend's or family member's small business works well, with
  their permission. Platforms often suspend accounts that advertise made-up businesses.
- **What you need:** A real website with a privacy policy page, working conversion tracking,
  and the owner's agreement on budget.
- **Budget:** $5–$10 a day for 14 days is enough to learn from. That's $70–$140 per platform.
- **Expect:** Small budgets produce small, noisy numbers. The goal is to learn the workflow,
  not to prove a campaign works.
