# How to study

This course takes 8 weeks at 1 hour a day, 6 days a week. You don't need any marketing
background. You need curiosity, a laptop, and a Google account.

## What performance marketing is, in one paragraph

Performance marketing is advertising where every dollar is measured against a result you
can count: a sale, a lead, a sign-up. You pay platforms such as Google and Meta to show ads,
you track what people do after they see them, and you move money toward what works. The job
is part analyst, part copywriter, part scientist. This course teaches all three parts.

## Your daily hour

Monday to Friday, do one lesson:

| Time | Step | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 25 min | **Learn:** Read `lesson.md`, then one link from its **Learn more** section | The lesson folder |
| 30 min | **Practice:** Do the task in `practice.md` and answer its **Check yourself** questions | The lesson folder |
| 5 min | **Notes:** Copy the notes template and fill it in | `my-work/notes/` |

## Your week

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | One lesson a day |
| Saturday | Weekly review. In the last week of each phase, do the checkpoint project instead. |
| Sunday | Rest |

A checkpoint project can take 60–90 minutes. That's expected.

## Tracking progress

The checklist in the main [README](../../README.md) is the only tracker. Tick a lesson when
you finish its practice, not when you finish reading.

## When you fall behind

- **Missed one day:** Do that lesson on Saturday and keep the review short.
- **Missed several days:** Pick up where you stopped. Don't skip lessons to catch up with the
  calendar. A week late with full understanding beats on time with gaps.
- **Practice taking longer than 30 minutes:** Stop at 45 minutes. Write what's left in your
  notes and finish it on Saturday.

## How to make it stick

- **Answer before you peek:** Each practice ends with questions. Answer them in your notes
  first, then expand the hidden answer. Getting one wrong and seeing why teaches more than
  reading the answer.
- **Explain it in plain words:** In your notes, explain the day's main idea as if to a friend with no
  marketing background. If you can't, reread that section.
- **Start each week with last week:** On Monday, spend 5 minutes answering last week's review
  questions from memory.
- **Do the maths by hand first:** Calculate metrics with a calculator before you let a
  spreadsheet do it. You need to feel what the numbers mean.

## When you get stuck

1. Reread the lesson's **Key ideas** section.
2. Look up the term in the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md) or the
   [metrics cheat sheet](../reference/metrics-cheatsheet.md).
3. Search the platform's help center. The [resources page](../reference/resources.md) lists
   the main ones.
4. Ask an AI assistant to explain it a different way. Check anything it claims about a
   platform's settings against the official help center, because those change often.
5. Still stuck? Write the question in your notes under **Open question** and move on. Bring it
   to someone who works in marketing.

## A word about platforms changing

Ad platforms rename buttons and features often. This course teaches the ideas behind them,
which change slowly. If a screen doesn't match a lesson, look for the same idea under a
different name, and search the help center.
