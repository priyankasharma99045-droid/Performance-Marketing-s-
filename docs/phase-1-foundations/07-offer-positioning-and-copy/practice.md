# Practice 7: Write the offer and first ad copy

**30 minutes** · **You need:** Your personas from practice 6 · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/lesson-07.md`

## Goal

Write the offer, positioning, and first headlines for your practice business.

## Steps

1. **Offer:** Write all five parts: what they get, price, risk reversal, bonus, and reason to
   act now. If your business is real, use real details. If not, make sensible ones up and
   mark them as assumptions.
2. **Positioning:** Write one positioning statement using the template from the lesson.
3. **USPs:** Write 3 USPs. Make each specific and provable.
4. **Benefit ladder:** Pick one feature and run the "so what?" test until you reach the real
   benefit. Write each step.
5. **Headlines:** Write 5 headlines of 30 characters or fewer. Use `=LEN(A1)` in a sheet to
   count characters. Make at least one answer an objection from your personas.
6. **Primary text:** Write 2 Meta-style primary texts of about 125 characters, one with PAS and
   one with BAB.

## Done when

- [ ] The offer has all five parts.
- [ ] There's one positioning statement and 3 USPs.
- [ ] 5 headlines are written, each 30 characters or fewer.
- [ ] 2 primary texts are written, one PAS and one BAB.

## Check yourself

1. Turn this feature into a benefit: "Our app has 500 recipes."

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Many answers work, such as "Never wonder what's for dinner again" or "A new dinner idea
   every night for over a year". The benefit is what the customer gets: less stress, more
   variety.
   </details>

2. Which part of an offer removes the fear of making a mistake?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Risk reversal: guarantees, free returns, free trials, or one-click cancellation.
   </details>

3. Rewrite "Fast delivery" as a specific USP.

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   For example, "At your door in 3 days" or "Order by 2 pm, delivered tomorrow". Use a real
   number you can prove.
   </details>

4. What do the letters in PAS stand for?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Problem, agitate, solve.
   </details>
