# Lesson 8: Competitor research

**Phase 1 · Week 2 · Day 3** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

Your competitors are spending money to learn what works in your market, and much of their
advertising is public. Studying it shows you which messages, offers, and formats the market
responds to, and where there's a gap you can own.

## Key ideas

### Free tools for seeing competitors' ads

| Tool | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) | Active ads on Facebook and Instagram, with start dates and versions |
| [Google Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com/) | Search, display, and YouTube ads by advertiser |
| [LinkedIn Ad Library](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/) | Ads that companies run on LinkedIn |
| [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/) | Top-performing ads and trends on TikTok |
| Google Search itself | Search your main keywords and see who advertises at the top |

### The longevity signal

Advertisers turn off ads that lose money. An ad that has run for months is **probably**
profitable. Look at the **Started running on** date in the Meta Ad Library. Many versions of
the same ad suggest the advertiser is testing around a winner.

This is a signal, not proof. Some brands leave ads on out of habit, and you can't see their
results.

### What to capture for each ad

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| **Angle** | What's the main idea? Price, quality, speed, status, fear of missing out? |
| **Hook** | What grabs attention in the first line or first 2 seconds? |
| **Offer** | Discount, free trial, free shipping, bundle, guarantee? |
| **Format** | Image, video, carousel? Polished or phone-shot? |
| **Call to action** | Shop now, learn more, sign up? |
| **Landing page** | Where does the click go, and does it match the ad? |
| **Running since** | How long has it been live? |

### Patterns and gaps

After 10 or more ads, look for:

- **Patterns:** If every competitor leads with price, the market expects it. You might need to
  match it, or deliberately stand out with a different angle.
- **Gaps:** An objection nobody answers, a persona nobody speaks to, or a format nobody uses.
  Gaps are your opportunity.

### Swipe file

Marketers keep a **swipe file**: a collection of ads worth learning from. Screenshot good ads,
note why they work, and come back when you need ideas. Learn from the idea, but never copy
another brand's ad.

## Example

Pebble searches the Meta Ad Library for three coffee subscription brands and finds:

- All three lead with discounts: "50% off your first bag".
- Two of three use polished studio photos.
- None mentions how fresh the beans are, or how quickly you can cancel.

**Pattern:** Discounts are expected. **Gap:** Freshness and one-click cancellation, which
matches Pebble's research from lesson 6. Pebble tests "Roasted this week, cancel in one
click" with phone-shot video.

## Common mistakes

- **Copying competitors:** You end up sounding like everyone else.
- **Assuming every ad works:** Only long-running ads carry a signal.
- **Looking only at ads:** The landing page and offer behind the ad matter as much.

## Learn more

Pick one (about 10 minutes):

- Explore the [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) for a brand you like. Find their oldest active ad.
- Explore the [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/) top ads for your industry.
