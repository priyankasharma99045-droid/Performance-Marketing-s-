# Phase 1: Foundations

**Weeks 1–2** · 10 lessons, 1 weekly review, 1 checkpoint

## What you'll be able to do

- Explain what performance marketing is and how it differs from brand marketing.
- Map a customer funnel and find where it leaks.
- Calculate every core metric, and work out what a business can afford per customer.
- Analyze campaign data in a spreadsheet without common mistakes.
- Research customers and competitors, and turn the findings into an offer and ad copy.
- Audit a landing page and prioritize improvements.

## Week 1 review

**Saturday** · 30–45 minutes · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/week-01-review.md`, using
[`templates/weekly-review.md`](../../templates/weekly-review.md)

Answer from memory first, then check against the lessons.

1. In two sentences, explain performance marketing to a friend.
2. What's the difference between capturing demand and creating demand? Give a channel example
   of each.
3. Draw the funnel with its four stages. Where would a retargeting ad sit?
4. Write `CPA = ? ÷ ?` and explain the two ways to lower CPA.
5. A business has a 50% gross margin and a $60 AOV. What are its break-even ROAS and
   break-even CPA?
6. Why is a 2.5 ROAS a loss for a business with a 30% margin?
7. Why must ratios be calculated from totals, not averaged?
8. Look at your practice 3 answers. Which Pebble campaign would you investigate first, and
   why?

Then catch up on any unfinished practice from the week.
