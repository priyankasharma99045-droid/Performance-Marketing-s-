# Lesson 2: Funnel and customer journey

**Phase 1 · Week 1 · Day 2** · 25 minutes

## Why this matters

People rarely buy the first time they see an ad. They move through stages, and each stage
needs a different message. The funnel is the map you use to decide which ad to show to whom,
and where you're losing people.

## Key ideas

### AIDA

The oldest model of buying, and still useful:

1. **Attention:** They notice you.
2. **Interest:** They want to know more.
3. **Desire:** They want what you offer.
4. **Action:** They buy or sign up.

### The marketing funnel

Marketers usually group the journey into stages. The shape is a funnel because fewer people
reach each stage.

| Stage | Also called | The person is thinking | Example ad goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Top of funnel (TOFU) | "I didn't know this existed." | Get attention, get video views |
| **Consideration** | Middle of funnel (MOFU) | "Is this right for me? What are my options?" | Get site visits, show proof |
| **Conversion** | Bottom of funnel (BOFU) | "Should I buy now? Is it safe?" | Get the sale or lead |
| **Loyalty** | Retention | "Should I buy again? Should I tell friends?" | Repeat purchase, referrals |

### Funnel maths

Every stage loses people. That's normal. Your job is to find the stage that loses the most and
fix it.

| Stage | People | Step rate |
|---|---|---|
| Saw the ad | 100,000 | |
| Clicked | 2,000 | 2% of viewers |
| Added to cart | 200 | 10% of clicks |
| Bought | 60 | 30% of carts |

Overall, 60 out of 100,000 viewers bought: 0.06%. Small numbers like this are normal in
advertising.

### Real journeys are messy

The funnel is a simplification. A real customer might see an Instagram ad on their phone,
forget about it, search the brand on their laptop a week later, read reviews, leave, get a
retargeting ad, and finally buy. That's 4 touchpoints on 2 devices. Measuring journeys like
this is hard. Lesson 20 covers how platforms try.

### E-commerce funnels compared with lead generation funnels

| | E-commerce | Lead generation |
|---|---|---|
| **Example** | Online coffee store | Dental clinic, insurance, software |
| **Conversion** | Online purchase | Form fill, call, or booking |
| **Value known at conversion?** | Yes, the order value | No. The sale happens later, offline. |
| **Main risk** | Abandoned carts | Low-quality leads that never buy |
| **Main metric** | ROAS or CPA | Cost per lead, and cost per qualified lead |

With lead generation, a cheap lead that never becomes a customer is worth nothing. You need to
track what happens after the form.

### Micro-conversions and macro-conversions

- **Macro-conversion:** The main goal. A purchase or a lead.
- **Micro-conversion:** A smaller step that signals interest. Viewing a product, adding to
  cart, starting a form, watching most of a video.

Micro-conversions help when you don't have enough macro-conversions to judge an ad.

### Match the message to the stage

- **Cold audience (awareness):** Lead with the problem or the benefit. Don't push a hard sale.
- **Warm audience (consideration):** Show proof: reviews, comparisons, how it works.
- **Hot audience (conversion):** Remove the last doubts: guarantee, free shipping, a reason to
  act now.

## Example

Brightsmile Dental, a fictional clinic, wants new patients:

| Stage | What the person thinks | Ad and channel | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | "My teeth look yellow in photos." | Instagram video on whitening results | Video views, reach |
| Consideration | "Is whitening safe? What does it cost?" | Blog post with prices and FAQs, promoted on Meta | Landing page views |
| Conversion | "Which clinic near me?" | Google Search ad for "teeth whitening near me" | Booking form leads |
| Loyalty | "Time for a checkup?" | Email reminder every 6 months | Repeat bookings |

## Common mistakes

- **Asking strangers to buy right away:** A hard-sell ad to a cold audience usually performs
  badly.
- **Judging awareness ads on sales alone:** They feed the later stages.
- **Counting all leads as equal:** In lead generation, track lead quality, not only volume.
