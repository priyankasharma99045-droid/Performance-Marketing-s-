# Practice 2: Map two funnels

**30 minutes** · **You need:** A calculator · **Save to:** `my-work/notes/lesson-02.md`

## Goal

Map the funnel for an online store and a lead generation business, and find where a funnel
leaks.

## Steps

### Part 1: Map two funnels (20 minutes)

1. Pick two businesses: one online store and one service that collects leads. Use real ones
   you know, or use Pebble Coffee Co. and Brightsmile Dental from the lessons.
2. For each, fill in this table in your notes:

   | Stage | What the customer thinks | What they do | Channel and ad idea | Metric to watch |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Awareness | | | | |
   | Consideration | | | | |
   | Conversion | | | | |
   | Loyalty | | | | |

3. For each business, write one sentence: What happens after the conversion that the
   business needs to track?

### Part 2: Find the leak (10 minutes)

Pebble Coffee Co. had this week:

| Stage | People |
|---|---|
| Saw an ad | 200,000 |
| Clicked | 3,000 |
| Viewed a product page | 900 |
| Added to cart | 150 |
| Bought | 60 |

1. Calculate the step rate for each stage: people at this stage ÷ people at the stage before.
2. Calculate the overall rate: buyers ÷ people who saw an ad.
3. Name the step that loses the biggest share of people, and suggest one reason why.

## Done when

- [ ] Two funnel tables are complete.
- [ ] All four step rates and the overall rate are calculated.
- [ ] You named the leakiest step and a possible cause.

## Check yourself

1. What are the step rates in Part 2?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   - Clicked: 3,000 ÷ 200,000 = 1.5% (this is the CTR)
   - Viewed a product page: 900 ÷ 3,000 = 30%
   - Added to cart: 150 ÷ 900 = 16.7%
   - Bought: 60 ÷ 150 = 40%
   - Overall: 60 ÷ 200,000 = 0.03%
   </details>

2. Which step loses the biggest share, and what might cause it?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Clicked to product page: 70% of people who clicked never reached a product page. Possible
   causes: the landing page is slow, it doesn't match the ad, or it doesn't link clearly to
   products. A 1.5% CTR is fine for social ads, so the click step is not the main problem.
   </details>

3. Why is cost per lead alone a risky metric for a dental clinic?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Leads can be cheap but low quality. If most never book or never show up, the clinic pays
   for nothing. Track how many leads become patients.
   </details>

4. A cold audience sees an ad that says "Buy now, 10% off today only". Why might it perform
   badly?

   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Cold audiences don't know or trust the brand yet. They need a reason to care before a
   reason to buy now.
   </details>
