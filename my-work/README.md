# My work

Your notes, practice output, and projects live here. The course material in `docs/` stays
untouched, so anyone reviewing your progress can look in one place.

| Folder | What goes in it | File naming |
|---|---|---|
| `notes/` | One note per lesson, from `templates/lesson-notes.md`, plus weekly reviews | `lesson-01.md`, `week-01-review.md` |
| `projects/` | The four checkpoint projects | Named in each checkpoint |

## Spreadsheets and screenshots

- **Spreadsheets:** Keep them in your Google Drive course folder. Paste the link into that
  lesson's note.
- **Screenshots:** Save them to `my-work/notes/img/`. Never include card numbers, billing
  pages, or other people's personal data.
