# Metrics practice

Started: 2026-10-17

Extra practice alongside the daily lessons, so the chapter 3 and 4 metrics stay fluent: glance
at a set of numbers, work out the relationships in your head, and name what's wrong. It takes
about an hour a week.

| Practice | When | What it builds |
|---|---|---|
| [1. Formulas from memory](#1-formulas-from-memory) | Before each lesson, a few minutes | Recall of every formula |
| [2. Metric cases](#2-metric-cases) | Once a week, 45–60 minutes | Mental calculation and diagnosis |

## 1. Formulas from memory

Before each lesson, write these 20 formulas on a blank page from memory. Check them against
this list, and say any you missed out loud once.

```
Chapter 3
CTR  = clicks ÷ impressions
CPC  = spend ÷ clicks
CPM  = spend ÷ impressions × 1,000
CVR  = conversions ÷ clicks
CPA  = spend ÷ conversions
ROAS = revenue ÷ spend
CPA  = CPC ÷ CVR

Chapter 4
Gross profit      = revenue − COGS
Gross margin      = gross profit ÷ revenue
Break-even ROAS   = 1 ÷ gross margin
Break-even CPA    = AOV × gross margin
Target CPA        = break-even CPA − profit wanted per order
Target ROAS       = AOV ÷ target CPA
LTV               = AOV × orders per year × years × gross margin
CAC               = all marketing spend ÷ new customers
LTV to CAC        = LTV ÷ CAC (3 or more is healthy)
Payback period    = months of gross profit to earn back CAC
Value of one lead = gross profit per customer × close rate
Profit after ads  = gross profit − ad spend
Discounted AOV    = AOV × (1 − discount)
```

- **Fewer sessions:** after a full week of 20 out of 20, do it twice a week. After another
  perfect week, do it once a week.
- **Back to daily:** if you miss more than one.

## 2. Metric cases

Once a week, take a set of metrics, work out the numbers in your head, and say out loud what's
wrong. Then check the answer. The cases and answers are in [metric cases](metric-cases.md).

Read every case in the same order:

1. Compare each number with last week or the target.
2. Find the first metric that changed along the chain: CPM → CTR → CPC → CVR → CPA → ROAS.
3. Name the likely cause from the **What moves it** column in
   [lesson 3](../../docs/phase-1-foundations/03-core-metrics/lesson.md).
4. Check whether the result is above or below break-even.

| Weeks | What the cases look like |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Round numbers, and only one metric changes |
| 3–5 | Real-looking numbers, including Pebble's practice data, where you calculate to find the problem |
| 6 | Traps: averaged ratios, discounts, cheap leads that don't convert |
| 7 onward | A fresh set of 8 from Claude, or a set you last did 4 or more weeks ago. Add metrics from later chapters as you reach them. |

## What to expect

- **Formulas:** these usually stick after 3–4 weeks of daily recall.
- **Spotting problems in meetings:** this takes about 2–3 months of weekly cases.
- **Busy week:** keep the formula recall and skip that week's cases.

Lessons 25, 28, 35, and 38–40 use these metrics heavily, and the weekly cases prepare you for
them.
